1. Requirement / question
What exactly needs to be found out.

2. Why it matters
The operational or analytical reason the request exists.

3. Priority / urgency
How fast the answer is needed and how important it is.

4. Scope constraints
Time range, language, geography, source types, or topics.

5. Specific information requirements
The narrower sub-questions or indicators that would help answer the main requirement. Army collection-management material describes this refinement step as breaking broad requirements into more actionable indicators and specific information requirements.

6. Desired output
What form the answer should take: summary, lead sheet, memo, alert, entity list, and so on.
