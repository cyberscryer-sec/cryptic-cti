Sample RFI

RFI ID: RFI-001
Title: Multilingual collection support for credential-theft ecosystem monitoring
Priority: High
Requested by: Threat Intelligence / Collections Support
Date required: 72 hours from tasking
Languages: English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese
Time window: Previous 30 days
Source scope: Lawful public and simulated closed-source-style text datasets used for collection support testing

Primary requirement
Identify and normalize English- and Chinese-language mentions relevant to the credential-theft ecosystem, with emphasis on infostealer tooling, stealer-log sales, actor handles, and associated infrastructure clues that may support further collection or analytic follow-up.

Why this request matters
Credential theft and dark-web exposure of compromised information remain a practical intelligence target because they can indicate active criminal monetization, access-broker activity, and downstream intrusion risk. CrowdStrike’s public material explicitly ties dark web monitoring to leaked credentials, stolen information, and malicious sharing and sale activity, which makes this a credible collection-support scenario.

Specific information requirements

What English- or Chinese-language references to infostealer malware families or “logs” sales appear in the scoped dataset?
Which actor handles, aliases, or seller identifiers appear repeatedly across relevant items?
Are there observable language variants, slang terms, or alternate spellings that should be normalized to the same entity or activity type?
Are there domains, URLs, wallets, contact methods, or platform references that should be captured as supporting infrastructure clues?
Which leads appear most relevant for follow-up collection, and which appear low-confidence, duplicated, or spam-like?

Desired output
Provide a short collection-response memo containing:

a one-paragraph executive summary,
a normalized entity table,
notable alias/variant mappings across English and Chinese,
source/relevance notes,
and recommended next collection steps or intelligence gaps.

Assumptions / Caveats
This request is for collection support and lead normalization, not final attribution. Analyst judgment is required to distinguish original reporting from recycled, spammed, or low-credibility content. That distinction matters because closed-source monitoring often produces high volume and repeated noise, and operational value comes from filtering that noise into useful finished outputs
